Section A — daily

One x-axis slot per calendar day, 1 Oct – 11 Nov 2026. Days with no published figure stay empty.

Section B — weekly

Weeks run Monday–Sunday. Crowd size shows the peak day; arrests and injuries are summed. * marks weeks clipped by the window.

Section C — cross-episode, one panel per movement

Seven episodes, four series each, on a single logarithmic count axis shared by every panel — so a point sits at exactly the number its tooltip reports, and panels can be compared by eye. Panel position names the movement; hue names the series, once, in the legend below. Hollow markers = regional, partial, derived or stock figure. Hover a series (or its legend entry) to trace it across all seven panels.

x — whole weeks since each movement's first action, true scale, so durations differ (Nahel 1 week, Gilets jaunes 43) and each panel keeps its own x extent. y — count, log scale, one range for all seven panels (about 0.5 → 7M); tick labels appear only in the left column, gridlines everywhere, and on a log axis equal vertical distance means equal proportional change wherever a series sits. Dashed segment = at least one week with no published figure lies between the two points. "obs" under each strip = weeks with a figure for protestors · arrests · forces injured · protestors injured.

Section C′ — cross-episode, transposed

The same data with the comparison axis flipped: one panel per series, one line per movement, direct labels instead of a legend. Colour encodes the movement on a single sequential ramp, light → dark in the same order in every panel, so the ramp is learned once. Use this to ask "did arrests behave the same way in every episode?".

x is capped at week 7 — the last week on which any movement has an arrest or injury figure (CPE, 28 Mar – 2 Apr 2006: 600 arrests). Gilets jaunes' enforcement series stops at Act IV (week 3) while its crowd series runs to Act 44 (week 43); letting that set the axis left every enforcement panel empty after week 4. A trailing + marks a series that continues past the cap — the untruncated curves are in Section C, where each panel keeps its own extent. y limits are per panel because the four series sit up to three orders of magnitude apart; the range is printed under each strip, so compare shape and percentage change here, not level.

Sources

Compiled by an LLM from the reports below; no figure here is a primary measurement made for this page. Where a movement has an official count, the interior ministry is the source of the arrest and officer-injury numbers and the unions publish the competing turnout figure. Counts that only ever existed as scattered daily press reports were summed by hand, which is where most of the error will be.

The 2026 movement — 1 Oct to 11 Nov

  • Fondation IFRAP — « Violences des 17 et 18 octobre 2026 : une facture qui approche le milliard d’euros » 21 Oct 2026 — the €247M–872M damage range for 17–18 Oct: ~€910M material damage and ~€190M lost turnover in the high estimate, €666M of it retail.
  • Le Monde — « Plus de 2 500 commerces dégradés », d’après CCI France 27 Oct 2026 — CCI France's shop counts: 2,488 hit on 17 Oct, of which 1,161 pillaged, sacked or burnt.
  • franceinfo — « Plus de 2 500 magasins touchés » 27 Oct 2026 — the 24 Oct increment (50 shops, total 2,538) and the 80% centre-town turnover drop.
  • BFMTV — CCI France, 20 shops vandalised on 31 Oct 1 Nov 2026 — why week 6 carries no damage figure: the count collapses after 24 Oct.
  • Capital.fr — « Combien ont coûté les 70 000 policiers de samedi » 28 Oct 2026 — the €15,000–30,000 per CRS company per day and €3.4M per 3,000 gendarmes benchmarks behind the policing-cost band; also the 1,435 arrests and 53 officers injured on 24 Oct.
  • Le Figaro — « Le coût exorbitant d’un week-end d’émeutes » 20 Oct 2026 — cross-check on the damage order of magnitude.
  • Le Canard enchaîné — « Les manifestants ont le mauvais œil » 10 Nov 2026 — Prof. Laurent Thines' (CHU de Besançon) press-and-blog register: 79 serious injuries in three weeks — 10 blinded, 9 cranio-facial, 7 amputations, 1 death. The source of the protester-injury floor, and the reason no weekly protester-injury series exists.
  • Ministère de l’Intérieur — origin of every arrest, custody and officer-injury count and of the ministry turnout figure. Published as press briefings and evening situation reports, not as a downloadable series, so the daily values were collected from the reporting above rather than from a dataset.

  • France 24 — « Bloquons tout », direct du 10 septembre 2025 10 Sep 2025 — 197,000 participants, 596 rallies, 253 blockades, 540 arrests (211 in Paris) — the final ministry point of the evening, superseding the 175,000 of 17:45.
